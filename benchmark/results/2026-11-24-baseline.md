# Backend baseline, 2026-11-24

Local baseline on one machine: backend in Docker with 2 CPUs and `--network host`, fake providers and page reader with 100 ms latency, 1000 users with 20 sorzs each (20 000 entries), a clean `sorzs.db` for every pass. Not production figures. Detail per pass: the other files in this folder.

## What the numbers say

1. **`sorzs.census` is the weakest operation.** With 20 000 entries each call loads every list and holds the only thread for about a second. Alone at 50 virtual users it never answers in time (93 of 105 calls hit the 60 s timeout). In the mixed scenario at 0.2 % of the traffic, about ten calls in two minutes freeze every other operation for 2 to 5 seconds: p95 of `health.check` is 2.7 s.
2. **Login and `users.add` cap at about 6 per second.** `Bun.password.hashSync` holds the thread for about 160 ms per hash, so 50 virtual users queue for a median of 6 s. Any burst of logins, such as a morning peak, is felt by all users.
3. **The rest is fast.** `sorzs.list`, `users.list`, `users.setRole` and `health.check` answer with p95 under 40 ms at 50 virtual users. `sorzs.search`, `sorzs.inspect` and `sorzs.add` sit at 100 to 200 ms, almost all of it the fake 100 ms latency of the provider.
4. **One thread.** The container never used more than about one of its two CPUs outside the hashing bursts (CPU avg 61 % of 200 % in the mixed medium pass). A second process would double the ceiling, the same way a smaller census would lift it.
5. **Saturation of the mixed scenario.** With the census in the mix it saturates before 1000 virtual users: at the very high profile it served about 57 requests per second in total, with p50 between 5 and 11 s. The stress profile stopped itself within seconds, when p95 passed 1 s.

## Limits of these figures

- The mixed weights are my assumption, with the census at 0.2 %.
- The census at 20 000 entries hides the ceiling of everything else. A mixed pass without the backoffice operations would show the ceiling of search and list alone; it has not been run.
- The fake providers answer at a fixed 100 ms. The real ones vary and can fail.
- k6 and the container share one machine.
